Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our 3D AI Visualizer & Stone Services

How does the 3D AI Room Visualizer work?

Our proprietary spatial AI engine analyzes your room photo, detects floor and wall boundaries, and realistically renders authentic Makrana marbles, granites, and tiles with real-time lighting, shadows, and perspective adjustment.

Are the slab quantity & cost calculations accurate?

Yes! The visualizer computes exact surface square footage and estimates the precise number of marble slabs or tile blocks required for your space, eliminating guesswork and costly material waste.

Where is UMATRADERSS based and do you ship pan-India?

Our main operational studio is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan — India's premier hub for stone architecture. We provide pan-India delivery with a zero-damage transport guarantee for all stone orders.

Can I request a custom stone consultancy for my project?

Absolutely. You can click "Consult a Specialist" or contact our Jaipur team via WhatsApp at +91 7852040620. We will assist you with virtual renders, physical quarry samples, and custom pricing.

Can I use the visualizer on my smartphone or tablet?

Yes, our AI visualizer works seamlessly across all devices including smartphones, tablets, and desktop workstations without requiring any app installation.
