About Us

Our mission is to empower homeowners, architects, and designers with spatial AI technology to visualize and select luxury natural stones with absolute precision.

Our Story

Who We Are

Founded in Jaipur, Rajasthan, UMATRADERSS is a modern spatial AI startup built to transform the natural stone and tile industry with real-time 3D room visualization technology.

Mission

Our mission is to eliminate guesswork in stone procurement by providing real-time 3D room visualization, precise slab calculations, and transparent quarry pricing.

Company Values

We are committed to uncompromised quality, quarry-direct transparency, authentic Indian and Italian marble craftsmanship, and zero-waste material estimation.
