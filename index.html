about us

Tell Me About Yourself

↓

Operating from Jaipur, Rajasthan — India’s historical capital of stone architecture — UMATRADERSS combines three decades of natural stone expertise with state-of-the-art 3D spatial AI technology. We specialize in sourcing pristine Makrana marbles, premium granites, and handcrafted architectural stone, delivering uncompromising luxury to homes, hotels, and commercial developments nationwide.

Our proprietary real-time 3D AI Visualizer service revolutionizes how stone is experienced. By enabling architects, interior designers, and homeowners to virtually apply authentic slab textures directly onto room photos, we eliminate design doubts, calculate exact slab area requirements, and provide instant tile block cost estimates — delivering a zero-waste, seamless digital workflow from quarry to construction site.

Common Questions

1

What materials can I preview in real-time 3D?

You can preview our complete collection of authentic Makrana marbles, premium Italian granites, onyx, quartzites, and custom tile patterns directly inside your room photo with realistic spatial lighting.

2

How does the AI room visualizer engine work?

Our spatial AI engine analyzes your room geometry, separates floors and walls, and realistically renders selected stone textures while preserving ambient lighting, natural shadows, and perspective grid alignment.

3

How accurate are slab quantity and tile cost calculations?

The visualizer computes exact surface square footage, factors in cut wastage ratios, and provides accurate tile block quantities and project budget estimates to prevent material over-purchasing.

4

Do I need technical knowledge or app installation to work with you?

Not at all! Our visualizer works instantly in your standard web browser on mobile, tablet, or desktop. Our Jaipur studio team manages all digital setups and custom stone consultancies for you.

5

Where is UMATRADERSS based and do you ship pan-India?

Our main operational studio and warehouse are located in Jaipur, Rajasthan. We ship pristine Makrana marble and granite slabs directly to construction sites across India with a zero-damage transport guarantee.

6

Can I request physical quarry samples before placing an order?

Yes, after visualizing your room online, you can request physical stone sample blocks dispatched directly from our Jaipur warehouse to inspect vein patterns, texture, and polish in person.

7

How do I request a custom stone consultancy for my space?

Click "Consult a Specialist" or contact us on WhatsApp (+91 7852040620). Our stone specialists will review your floor plans, generate high-resolution 3D renders, and guide your quarry selections.

8

Is my uploaded room photo secure and kept private?

Your uploaded room images are processed strictly in-memory for generating your visualizer render. We never store publicly, sell, or share your private property images.

9

What is the difference between Standard Mode and Pro Mode?

Standard Mode provides instant automated floor rendering. Pro Mode unlocks granular controls for grain orientation angles, pattern repeat scaling, exposure adjustment, and shadow depth control.

10

How quickly can stone slabs be delivered to my site?

Standard slab shipments from Jaipur typically arrive within 5 to 10 business days depending on location, complete with transit insurance and offloading support.
